Partners

Partners

DIVD Academy works with organizations and communities that help young people learn practical cybersecurity skills, build confidence online, and grow into responsible digital citizens.

Our partners

Hackshield

Cybercrime is rising, and this initiative helps people learn how to protect themselves and others online. It gives young learners the tools and mindset to become cyber agents.

Stichting Cyberbrein.nl

For young people who feel at home in the digital world and are curious about coding, security, and online creativity, this partner supports skills and digital confidence.

Cisco Networking Academy

Cisco Networking Academy helps learners, educators, and communities build technology skills, bridge the digital divide, and open doors to future careers in the digital economy.

The Dutch National Police

Working with the Dutch police supports awareness, safety, and digital resilience, while helping young people understand the role of law enforcement in a secure society.

The municipality of The Hague

The city supports digital inclusion, community strength, and a modern, connected society in which young people can safely grow their digital skills.

ESET

ESET brings decades of experience in digital security and supports the belief that strong protection and digital literacy are essential in everyday life.

SIDN Fund

SIDN Fund supports projects with societal value that strengthen the internet, improve digital literacy, and contribute to the public value of the online world.

Bit Academy

Bit Academy helps students explore ICT through hands-on learning, practical assignments, and team-based work in a modern technical education setting.

ECP

ECP shares our mission of keeping digital life secure and helps promote high standards of innovation, resilience, and digital trust.

Government of the Netherlands

The Dutch government plays a key role in supporting digital safety, civic resilience, and the broader public value of a safer and stronger internet.
