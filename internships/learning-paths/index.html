Learning paths

Alongside your project work you follow a learning path. The paths are built from our course catalogue and match common IT and cybersecurity study directions.

Not finding your direction?

If your study direction or specialization is not in the catalogue, talk to your Academy supervisor. Tailoring is usually possible: together we assemble a path that fits your program, your personal learning goals and the project you contribute to.
