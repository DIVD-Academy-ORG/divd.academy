Internships at DIVD Academy

An internship at DIVD Academy means working on real projects that make the digital world safer, with a practical supervisor beside you and a learning path that matches your study program. This section explains what to expect, how the process works and how to apply.

What to expect

Real projects

You join one of our running projects together with volunteers and professionals — not a made-up assignment on the side.

A practical supervisor

Every student has a practical supervisor and every project has a client you can reach directly. Progress and phase meetings are planned with you and your school.

Hybrid by default

We work partly on site in The Hague and partly remote. Your school's requirements are agreed up front so your internship fits your program.

Internship or traineeship?

An internship is the placement you do as part of a study program at a school, college or university, with a learning agreement and assessment forms. A traineeship is a mentored step after finishing Academy courses, outside a school program. If you are not sure which fits you, read the traineeships page or simply apply and mention your situation.
