From learning to doing

After completing Academy courses, learners can apply their skills on real projects and internships through DIVD.Works, a separate platform connecting trained talent with businesses and schools. DIVD Academy focuses on teaching; DIVD.Works focuses on practical placement — the two are connected but distinct organizations.
