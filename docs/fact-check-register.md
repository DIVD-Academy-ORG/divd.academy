# Fact-check register (issue #6)

Every factual claim on the public site that needs an internal source or an
accountable approver before launch. Compiled from `main` on 2026-09-22 (EN pages;
the NL pages are translations of the same claims unless noted).

**How to use:** the approver fills in *Source / approver* and *Status*
(`ok`, `change`, `remove`). A claim is only `ok` with a named source or person.
When all rows are `ok` or resolved, a named content approver signs off at the bottom.

Legend for *Review*: **P&C** = HR/People & Culture, **Legal** = privacy/legal,
**Partner** = the partner itself must agree, **Owner** = programme owner.
