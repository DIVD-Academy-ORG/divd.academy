FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to some of the most common questions about DIVD Academy. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to get in touch.

General

Who can join DIVD Academy?

DIVD Academy is aimed at young people interested in cybersecurity, ethical hacking and IT, generally with little to no prior experience required. Specific eligibility details may vary by course — see the courses page for the requirements of each course.

Are the courses free?

Most Academy courses are free to access. See the courses page for details on individual courses and any prerequisites.

How are courses delivered?

Courses combine self-paced online material with practical exercises. Formats can vary by course — check each course's page for specifics on structure and expected time commitment.

Traineeships and DIVD.Works

What is a traineeship?

A traineeship is a practical, mentored step after completing Academy courses, where you apply your skills to real work under the guidance of experienced mentors. See the traineeships page for more detail.

How does DIVD Academy relate to DIVD.Works?

DIVD Academy focuses on teaching; DIVD.Works focuses on connecting trained talent with practical placements and projects. They are connected but distinct organizations.

How do I apply for a traineeship?

You can apply directly via our application page. We recommend completing relevant Academy courses first.
