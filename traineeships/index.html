Traineeships

A DIVD Academy traineeship is a practical, mentored step for learners who have completed Academy courses and want to apply their skills on real work. Trainees are guided by experienced mentors while they build confidence in a supportive, structured environment.

What a traineeship involves

Mentored learning

Trainees work alongside experienced practitioners who provide feedback and guidance throughout the traineeship.

Practical work

Rather than theoretical exercises alone, trainees apply cybersecurity and ethical hacking skills to genuine tasks.

A path forward

Traineeships are designed as a bridge toward DIVD.Works, where trainees can take on further practical placements.

How it fits together

Courses build foundational knowledge; a traineeship turns that knowledge into practical, mentored experience; and DIVD.Works connects trainees with organizations that need trained talent. Each step builds on the one before it.
